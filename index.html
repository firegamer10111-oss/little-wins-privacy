Little Wins Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026
Developer: Hussain Mohsin, Oman
Contact: ranktubelol@gmail.com

Summary

Little Wins keeps everything on your device. We do not run accounts, we do not collect personal information about children or parents, and we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

  • A child's nickname (we ask for a nickname, not a real name), an optional age group (Toddler or Child) and the chosen buddy character.
  • Routines, tasks, completion history, stars, levels, virtual items, the chosen language and app settings.
  • An optional parent PIN, stored only as a salted hash.

This information stays in the app's local database on your device. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us. It is deleted when you delete a child's profile, use "Reset all data", or uninstall the app.

Device backups

Like most apps, the app's local data may be included in your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Android device backup to your Google account) if you have that turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google; we never receive them.

Backups you export

If you choose "Export backup", the app creates a file and opens your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email) is your choice and is governed by that service's terms.

Problem reports

If the app runs into an unexpected error, it keeps a short technical note about it on the device (the error message and where in the app it happened — never your child's information), at most the last 20. Nothing is sent automatically. A parent can view the count, choose to share the report with us (Settings → Diagnostics → Share), or clear it.

Purchases

Little Wins Premium is an optional subscription sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive your name, card details or payment information. The app only receives confirmation from the store that a subscription is active.

Notifications

If a parent turns on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on the device. No push service, device token or server is used.

Children

Little Wins is designed for children to use with a parent's involvement. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Purchases, settings and external links are behind a parent gate. There is no chat, no social features, no public profiles and no advertising. We aim to meet the requirements of children's privacy laws such as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR and Oman's Personal Data Protection Law — and because the app collects no personal data, no data is shared with anyone.

Permissions

Notifications (optional, asked only when a parent turns on reminders). The app does not access the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Your choices

Delete a child's profile in Parent area → Children, or all data in Settings → Reset all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all data from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
